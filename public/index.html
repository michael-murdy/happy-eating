Grilled Whole Hogfish on Citrus / Herb Bed

Ingredients

  • 1 whole hogfish, cleaned and scaled (about 2–3 lbs)
  • 1 lemon, thinly sliced
  • 1 orange, thinly sliced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Kosher salt and freshly cracked black pepper

Optional

  • 1 fresh herbs (thyme, rosemary, or a mix)

Preparation

  1. Prep the grill — Heat grill to medium-high (about 400°F). Clean and oil grates well.
  2. Season the fish — Pat hogfish dry. Rub lightly with olive oil, then season inside and out with salt and pepper.
  3. Stuff the cavity — Add a few citrus slices, garlic, and herbs inside the fish.
  4. Build the citrus bed — On a grill-safe pan or directly on grates, lay overlapping slices of lemon and orange. Place herbs and garlic over the citrus.
  5. Grill the fish — Lay the hogfish directly on top of the citrus/herb bed. Close the lid and cook 8–12 minutes per side (depending on thickness), until flesh flakes easily and internal temp reaches ~135–140°F. Use wide spatulas to gently flip if needed.

Serving

  1. Transfer the fish to a platter, keeping some of the charred citrus and herbs as garnish.
  2. Drizzle lightly with olive oil or a squeeze of fresh lemon juice.
  3. Serve family-style, letting diners pull flaky portions right from the bone.

Tip: Citrus bed prevents sticking and infuses subtle flavor. You can slice the fish crosswise before grilling for faster, more even cooking and extra flavor.

Roasted Pork Tenderloin with Red Wine, Mushroom Ragout

Ingredients

  • 2 pork tenderloins (about 1.5-2lbs total), trimmed of silver fat band
  • 8 oz whole, mini-portabello mushrooms (or white), cut in half, quartered, or sliced to preference
  • 1/2 medium-sized white onion, sliced
  • 4 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 3 sprigs rosemary, stems reserved (if brining)
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp kosher salt
  • 1/2 cup red wine (use a decent one you would drink)

Brine

Optional, but recommended

  • 2 qts water
  • 4 tbsp kosher salt
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • reserved rosemary stems

Brine (optional)

  • 5 mins prep, 2-3hrs rest
  1. Mix all brine ingredients together
  2. Stir until clear (i.e. completely dissolved)
  3. Add pork
  4. Refrigerate for 2-3 hours
  5. Remove from brine, rinse, dry with paper towels
  6. Let rest on rack to dry

Tip: You can let this rest on rack just prior to cooking or pre brine and rest on a rack over a cookie sheet in the fridge for up to 24hrs.
