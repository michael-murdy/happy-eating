Roasted Pork Tenderloin with Red Wine, Mushroom Ragout

September 4, 2025 - 2 minutes read - 372 words

Ingredients

  • 2 pork tenderloins (about 1.5-2lbs total), trimmed of silver fat band
  • 8 oz whole, mini-portabello mushrooms (or white), cut in half, quartered, or sliced to preference
  • 1/2 medium-sized white onion, sliced
  • 4 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 3 sprigs rosemary, stems reserved (if brining)
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp kosher salt
  • 1/2 cup red wine (use a decent one you would drink)

Brine

Optional, but recommended

  • 2 qts water
  • 4 tbsp kosher salt
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • reserved rosemary stems

Brine (optional)

  • 5 mins prep, 2-3hrs rest
  1. Mix all brine ingredients together
  2. Stir until clear (i.e. completely dissolved)
  3. Add pork
  4. Refrigerate for 2-3 hours
  5. Remove from brine, rinse, dry with paper towels
  6. Let rest on rack to dry

Tip: You can let this rest on rack just prior to cooking or pre brine and rest on a rack over a cookie sheet in the fridge for up to 24hrs.

Pork and Ragout

  1. Preheat oven to 350oF
  2. Prepare all ingredients prior to cooking
  3. Season meat generously with salt
  4. Heat pan over medium high heat, add oil and butter
  5. Sear pork on all sides
  6. Push meat to sides of pan. Add onions to middle.
  7. Stir and cook for a few minutes, until broken up and starting to become translucent.
  8. Add garlic and mushrooms. Stir to combine.
  9. Place in oven.
  10. Stir vegetables occasionally, every 10 minutes or so.
  11. Cook until pork internal temp is 130-135.
  12. Pull pork from pan, place in foil and cover tightly. Rest at least 15 minutes. Set pan aside.

Tip: You can fire the pork early, up to 2hrs before serving. Then wrap tightly with foil and then cover with dish towels for up to 2hrs prior to serving.

Finishing the Ragout

  1. Refire the pan with mushrooms, onions, garlic and remaining fat
  2. Reduce and sautee to desired texture.
  3. Add wine, reduce to a few tablespoons over high heat.
  4. Puncture foil wrap holding pork and pour excess liquid into pan.
  5. Reduce a few more minutes to desired texture.

Serving

  1. Slice pork (wait until it is no longer steaming after unwrapping from foil)
  2. Place onto serving dish.
  3. Gently pour over ragout liquid, careful to hold back vegetables.
  4. Pour remaining ragout into middle of pork or serve along side in separate bowl.
