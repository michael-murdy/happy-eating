Roasted Pork Tenderloin with Red Wine, Mushroom Ragout

September 4, 2025 - One minute read - 96 words

Ingredients

  • 2 pork tenderloins (about 1.5-2lbs total)
  • 8 oz whole, mini-portabello mushrooms (or white), cut in half, quarterd, or sliced to preference
  • 1/2 medium sized white onion, sliced
  • 4 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 3 sprigs rosemary, stems reserved
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 tbsp kosher salt (optional for brine)
  • 1 tbsp kosher salt (seasoning on meat)
  • 2 tbsp honey (optional, for brine)

Instructions

  1. Boil potatoes until just tender; drain.
  2. Smash gently; pan-fry in butter + oil until crispy.
  3. Season and serve.

Tip: Add minced garlic or rosemary in the last minute for extra aroma.
