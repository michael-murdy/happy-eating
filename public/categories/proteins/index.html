Roasted Pork Tenderloin

Ingredients

  • 1 lb baby potatoes
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt & pepper

Instructions

  1. Boil potatoes until just tender; drain.
  2. Smash gently; pan-fry in butter + oil until crispy.
  3. Season and serve.

Tip: Add minced garlic or rosemary in the last minute for extra aroma.
